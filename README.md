# Philippe Fontaine

**Data scientist & economist · French Senate · Paris, France**

I analyse economic, social and budgetary data to support public policy evaluation and parliamentary oversight. With over ten years of experience in the French public sector, I combine **quantitative analysis, public finance and application development** to turn complex data into useful evidence and tools.

Previously at the General Inspectorate of Finance (IGF), the French Treasury and Insee.

## What I work on

- **Policy evaluation and simulation:** econometrics, forecasting and microsimulation applied to taxation, healthcare and public spending. At IGF, I analysed several billion healthcare claims with R and Spark to simulate reforms to coverage for long-term conditions.
- **Fraud detection:** rules engines and web data collection for energy transition and international trade analysis, including a reference database of several million prices.
- **Decision-support tools:** Shiny applications for operational teams and interactive dashboards, including a government revenue dashboard used by the French Budget Directorate for the 2024 spending reviews.

My portfolio also lists my [contributions to public reports](https://www.philippefontaine.eu/index-fr.html#publications).
